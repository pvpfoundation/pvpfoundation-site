Recognizing Academic Excellence and Celebrating Independence Day

August 15, 2026

PVP Foundation proudly recognized students of the Irumbadi school for their academic performance, dedication, and commitment to learning. The recognition ceremony celebrated students who demonstrated consistent effort and excellence in their studies, encouraging them to continue pursuing their educational goals. As part of the recognition, students were presented with certificates and shields in appreciation of their achievements, along with sweets and stationery items as tokens of encouragement and support.

The occasion also marked India's Independence Day, which was celebrated enthusiastically at the school on August 15, 2026. Students, teachers, and members of the community came together to commemorate the nation's independence and celebrate the achievements of the school's young learners. The celebration brought together the spirit of national pride with the importance of recognizing and encouraging academic achievement.

PVP Foundation believes that recognizing students for their hard work can inspire confidence, encourage continued academic progress, and motivate other students to strive for excellence. The certificates, shields, sweets, and stationery presented to the students were small but meaningful gestures of appreciation, reminding them that their efforts are seen, valued, and supported by their community.

Honors Irumbadi Students

December 2025

The PVP Foundation held a landmark recognition ceremony in Irumbadi village, celebrating the achievements of students who demonstrated exceptional effort, discipline, and improvement in their academic pursuits.

The event was attended by local dignitaries, educators, and community members, highlighting the importance of recognizing and encouraging academic excellence in rural areas.

The ceremony featured inspiring speeches, award presentations, and a commitment to continue supporting the educational aspirations of Irumbadi's youth.
