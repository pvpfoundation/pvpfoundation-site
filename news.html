Honors Irumbadi students

December 2025

The PVP Foundation held a landmark recognition ceremony in Irumbadi village, celebrating the achievements of students who demonstrated exceptional effort, discipline, and improvement in their academic pursuits.

The event was attended by local dignitaries, educators, and community members, highlighting the importance of recognizing and encouraging academic excellence in rural areas.

The ceremony featured inspiring speeches, award presentations, and a commitment to continue supporting the educational aspirations of Irumbadi's youth.
