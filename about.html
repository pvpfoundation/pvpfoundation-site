Our story

The PVP Foundation was formed in honour of Pitchaiammal Veerabadran Ponnuswamy, reflecting deep respect for parents, ancestors, and community.

Origins

The foundation grew from a simple conviction: education transforms lives. The effort began in Irumbadi, where access to higher education has long been limited. Irumbadi is a small hamlet located along the banks of Vaigai River, about 30 km from Madurai. Surrounded by natural scenery, it remains a remote area with limited educational resources, and many students face logistical challenges when pursuing higher studies.

Visionary

Late Mr. Ponnuswamy, the first postgraduate from Irumbadi (M.Tech, IIT Madras), strongly believed in the power of education. Together with his brothers, the late Mr. Muthukrishnan and the late Mr. Ravi, he began encouraging village students by recognising academic achievement with small cash prizes and certificates starting in 1998.

Formation

In 2013, these efforts were formalised as the Pitchaiammal Veerabadran Ponnuswamy (PVP) Foundation. Dr. Dheenadhayalan, a Ph.D scholar in Biology from Irumbadi and currently serving as Director at AstraZeneca (USA), played a key role in registering the foundation. Mr. Muthuramanlingam, a Chemistry Professor at Kaumudi College, Tamil Nadu, provided academic and organisational support during formation.
